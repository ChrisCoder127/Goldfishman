Memory

Goldfish can learn routines and remember things for much longer than three seconds.

🌈

Colors

Goldfish come in orange, red, white, black, yellow and many combinations.

🌊

Origins

Goldfish were domesticated from wild carp in ancient China.

🐠

Personality

Goldfish can recognize feeding times and learn to associate people with food.
